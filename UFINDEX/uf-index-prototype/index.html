Messaging · WhatsApp vs SMS

Feasibility checked August 2026. Verdict: build WhatsApp in Phase 2, skip SMS entirely.

Do this · Phase 2

WhatsApp Business API

Reminders, results, and coach follow-ups all count as utility messages — the cheap tier. No DLT paperwork; only Meta business verification plus template approval, which takes days.

Utility (reminders, results)₹0.115 / msg
Authentication (OTP)₹0.115 / msg
Marketing₹0.8631 / msg
1,000 pilot users · weekly reminder≈ ₹460 / month

Utility messages sent inside an open 24-hour service window are free. Go through an Indian BSP (AiSensy, Gupshup, Interakt, MSG91) rather than Meta directly.

Skip

SMS

India's TRAI rules force every SMS sender through the DLT portal: principal-entity registration, sender ID approval, and every template pre-cleared. Weeks of paperwork for a worse product — no images, no buttons, no read receipts.

  • WhatsApp needs no DLT registration; SMS does.
  • Higher per-message cost for less capability.
  • Nothing in the app needs SMS that WhatsApp or push can't do better.

Phase 1 needs neither. The app already sends free local push notifications — weekly reminder and streak-at-risk — with zero cost and zero paperwork. When WhatsApp goes in, add a whatsapp_optin consent to the consent screen: under DPDP you cannot message people without it. One caution: templates must stay strictly utility-toned, or Meta reclassifies them as marketing at 7× the price.

Phase 1: you measure yourself. Phase 2: a coach joins you. Phase 3: AI insights lead — plus everything that makes UF Index a daily habit instead of a weekly form.

AI insights & coach chat

Claude-generated narratives replace the rule-based v1 insights — and become a conversation about your own data.

Why is my afternoon energy low?
Your last 3 check-ins show short sleep on weekdays — your 2 pm dip tracks it almost exactly. Want a wind-down plan?
The headline of Phase 3: one-way insights become a relationship — and warm users up for the human coach.
Wearable & health integrations

Apple Health, Google Fit, Oura, Fitbit, smart scales — sleep, steps, heart rate, weight and body comp flow in automatically.

Apple HealthGoogle FitOuraFitbitSmart scale

30-second weekly re-check

Measurements barely move week to week — keep them, only re-ask the pillars that change: energy, sleep, feeling.

2 questions · ~30 sec · wearables fill the rest
The weekly loop must feel lighter than the first run — that's what keeps streaks alive.
