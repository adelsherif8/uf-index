Who coaches whom

A hundred people sign up. There are five coaches. Try each way of splitting them and watch what it does to a cohort.

—

Each block is one organisation. When a coach's bar is one colour, one person understands that whole group. When it is five colours, nobody does.

Five things to decide

The strategy is the easy half, and it can be changed later. These cannot, once people are already with coaches.

Can a client ask for a particular coach? answer first

Not built. This matters more than the strategy: some clients will want a female coach, or someone who speaks their language. Asked after assignment, it becomes a transfer — and that costs them the relationship they had started.

Answer this before switching automatic assignment on. If clients can ask, the strategy becomes a default rather than a rule, and that is a different build.
What is a real caseload? the cap

Currently 25, which is a number I invented. It is a hard stop — a full coach is skipped, and if everyone is full the assignment refuses rather than quietly overloading someone.

Ask Parina and Manjula where coaching turns into admin. Their answer is the number.
When does someone get a coach? timing
  • On sign-up — everyone has a coach immediately, including people who never check in. Caseloads fill with ghosts.
  • On first check-in — only people who actually engaged.
  • Manually — you work the queue. Most control, stops scaling around fifty.
On first check-in. A caseload should be people, not sign-ups.
What if a cohort is bigger than one coach? overflow

Thirty-four Gurgaon Police clients do not fit in a caseload of 25. Drag the cap slider below 34 and watch that cohort break in two.

  • Split it — 25 to one coach, the rest to another.
  • Move the whole cohort to someone with room.
  • Raise that coach's cap — a deliberate, recorded exception.
Split, but always to the same second coach, so one station never has more than two.
Does anything rebalance on its own? drift

If Parina ends on 30 and Manjula on 5, does the system move people?

No. Moving a client mid-programme costs them their coach and gains you a tidier number. Show it on the Coaches screen and let a person decide.
